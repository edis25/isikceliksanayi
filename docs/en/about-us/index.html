Our History

A journey of more than half a century

Scroll to explore ↓
1965

Foundation

Işık Çelik started production in Karabük, the heart of the Turkish steel industry.

1970s

Years of Growth

The hot-rolled product range expanded; Işık Çelik became a trusted domestic supplier.

1990s

Modernization

Rolling lines were renewed; capacity and product quality were raised to international standards.

2000s

Opening to Export

First regular shipments to European and Middle Eastern markets; exports became central to our growth strategy.

2010s

Global Strength

With exports reaching 100+ countries across 5 continents, we became a global force of Turkish steel.

2024

Sustainability Investments

A 2.812 MWp rooftop solar power plant was installed at our existing facility.

2025

Next-Generation Facility

Our new facility came online: capacity tripled to 450,000 tons/year; with 3.991 MWp of new solar, total renewable power reached 6.803 MWp.

About Us

Reliability, Consistency and Excellence

With more than half a century of experience, Işık Çelik represents reliability, consistency and excellence in the steel industry.

Our manufacturing processes are carried out in accordance with international quality standards, focusing on sustainability, operational efficiency and continuous improvement. Through our advanced technological infrastructure and automation investments, we consistently deliver high-quality products with dependable performance.

Today, we proudly export to more than 100 countries across five continents, representing the strength of the Turkish steel industry in global markets.
